## Phase 3 — attack A: the cash-out study (CLEARS — holding vindicated, NO nomination)

**Instrument.** `scripts/exit_study.py` (self-test PASS: informative-exit fixture detected
p=0.0000; random-exit fixture flat p=0.59; event-clustering collapse). For each winner signal ×
each backer wallet in its atoms, reconstructs that wallet's BUY/SELL on the same
(condition_id, outcome_index) up to resolution from `trader_fills` (both sides, priced).

**1. Backers rarely exit.** Of 1163 (signal, backer) pairs: **4.82% had any pre-resolution SELL,
1.72% a majority-exit (≥50% of their buy)**. The sharps overwhelmingly HOLD to resolution — like
us. Of the 20 majority-exits, **15 exited in profit, 5 at a loss** (favorite drifted up ⇒ risk-off
profit-taking, not obviously information). Exit timing spreads across the whole hold window.

**2. Our held win rate by backer-exit bucket (event-clustered):** no exit **94.4%** (64 evts) ·
≥1 any-exit 81.7% (13 evts) · ≥1 majority-exit **81.8%** (11 evts). A backer bailing coincides
with a ~13pt lower held win rate.

**3. Counterfactual P&L (flat $100/signal, at-fire entry):** hold-to-resolution **+$3080** vs
mirror-their-exits **+$2985** (all 20 exits priced from SELL vwap, 0 unpriceable). **Δ = −$96:
mirroring the exits LOSES money** — the position still wins 82% of the time and you'd sell in
profit forgoing the larger resolution payout.

**4. Information test.** "≥1 backer majority-exit" vs matched non-exit: diff +12.6%, seeded
label-permutation **p = 0.0374 (raw)**. Real-ish — but on **only 11 exit-events, below the
pre-registered N≥20 floor ⇒ INDETERMINATE**, it enters the experimental Bonferroni/FDR family
(where a lone 0.037 does not survive), AND acting on it is P&L-negative (metric 3).

**A verdict: HOLDING VINDICATED. No exit-follow nomination** — the signal fails the N≥20 floor,
is not FDR-clean, and mirror-exit loses money. The honest read: sharp exits are rare, mostly
profit-taking, and following them would cost us. (Re-read trigger: revisit if majority-exit
events accrue past 20 with the difference persisting AND a priced mirror rule turns +EV.)
