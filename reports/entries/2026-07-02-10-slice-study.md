**Date:** 2026-07-02 · **Branch:** `feat/slice-study` (worktree off `main` cb35d7f, tag
`pre-slice-study-20260702`) · **Instrument:** `scripts/slice_study.py` · **Paper-only, no

Mission: map the entire forward record into pre-registered slices, measure each slice's
reliability (matched-blind surplus, selection-null, persistence) AND frequency
(qualifying events/day), rank by expected-$/day-with-confidence, and emit a binding
PRIORITIZE / NEUTRAL / DODGE table. Exploration nominates; forward data confirms; D7
promotes. Nothing here changes live behavior (K4).

### Data-honesty amendment discovered in Phase 0 (pre-registered handling)

The upsert (`upsert_consensus_signal`) overwrites `price_std`, `recency_mins`,
`total_usd`, `best_backer_rank`, `n_backers`, `n_opposers` on every 2-min cycle. The only
set-once at-fire columns are `initial_mean_price`, `initial_net_count`,
`initial_n_backers`, `first_detected_at`. Neither `consensus_snapshots` (net_count /
n_backers / prices only) nor `market_feature_log` (396 rows) recovers the rest.
Therefore each dimension is labeled by its definition source:

- **AT-FIRE-TRUE:** sport-regime, market type (title/slug are static), price band
  (`initial_mean_price`), net_count shape (`initial_net_count`), opposition
  (`initial_n_backers − initial_net_count`), time-of-day (`first_detected_at`), horizon
  (`resolved_at − first_detected_at`, with the standing caveat that `resolved_at` is
  processing time).
- **DRIFT-DEFINED (last-observed, not knowable at fire):** price σ, backer composition
  (elite present, best_backer_rank), freshness (`recency_mins`), liquidity (`total_usd`).

**Binding rule:** a DRIFT-DEFINED cell can never be a plain PRIORITIZE or DODGE. Its
verdict is reported with a `†` cap ("capture-gated") and its only allowed consequence is
a nomination to add at-fire capture of that column, then re-run. This is the K1/K2
posture applied to slice-definition drift.

### Per-cell metrics (all event-clustered on `COALESCE(event_slug, condition_id)`, all at-fire entry)

- **surplus** vs blind baseline matched on (regime × 5-band `width_bucket`) — never the
  global blind alone. Missing (regime,band) blind cell → band-only fallback, flagged.
- **selection-null p**: the `selection_null.py` machinery (imported, its CLI untouched),
  profile = (5-band × UTC-day × regime), ≥1000 seeded draws from the `_blind` universe,
  surpluses pre-adjusted by the same matched baseline.
- **realizable ROI** at measured costs: entry_exec = at-fire + **0.5¢** haircut, fee 2%;
  ROI/row = (won − entry_exec)/entry_exec − 0.02; event-clustered; 95% CI by seeded
  event-bootstrap (2,000 resamples).
- **freq** = qualifying events/day over the whole record AND over the last 48h of fires
  (freq_recent — the planning number; the World Cup is ending).
- **expected_$_per_day** = freq_recent × $100 × realizable_ROI, CI from the joint
  bootstrap (Poisson on the 48h count × ROI bootstrap).
- **persistence**: matched surplus positive in how many of the record's UTC days; and in
  how many regimes (for non-regime dims).
- **K2 stability**: cell surplus recomputed with the drifted `mean_price` entry; sign
  flip ⇒ UNSTABLE, never PRIORITIZE.
- **WC-exclusion**: soccer-driven cells recomputed without `fifwc%` rows (survives-ex-WC
  column).
- **leave-one-day-out**: verdict-bearing cells only; any single-day removal flipping the
  surplus sign ⇒ flagged FRAGILE.

### Multiplicity + verdicts (binding)

Benjamini-Hochberg FDR at **q = 0.10** across the full family's selection-null p-values.

- **PRIORITIZE** ⇔ FDR-surviving null ∧ realizable-ROI bootstrap LB > 0 ∧ N ≥ 20 events
  ∧ positive in ≥2 regimes-or-day-splits ∧ freq_recent ≥ 1 event/day ∧ not UNSTABLE ∧
  at-fire-true definition (else †-capped).
- **DODGE** ⇔ realizable-ROI bootstrap UB < 0 at N ≥ 20 (drift-defined ⇒ †-capped).
- **NEUTRAL / INDETERMINATE** otherwise. Small N is indeterminate, never dodge-by-noise.

Kill criteria K1–K4 as in the run brief (classifier coverage; drifted-vs-at-fire
stability; <5 FDR survivors ⇒ "no finer structure" IS the finding; nothing changes live
behavior).

Self-test (mandatory before any live reading is trusted): synthetic fixture with a known
injected edge in cell X and none in cell Y must PRIORITIZE X and leave Y NEUTRAL; a
pure-noise fixture must yield ~0 FDR survivors.

### DODGE (reliably losing at N≥20 — the mirror test)

| pop | cell | N | surplus | realizable ROI [95% CI] | ev/day | $/day |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|
| strict | regime=tennis | 110 | −0.1% | **−23.7% [−37.3, −8.7]** | 20.0 | −474 |
| strict | moneyline | 179 | +3.0% | **−13.7% [−25.8, −2.5]** | 37.0 | −507 |

**Mechanism (verified, band decomposition):** strict-tennis is a barbell. Bands 1–3
(entry 0.10–0.49, 86 events) carry the whole loss — band 1 hit **0% on 26 events**
(entry ≈ 0.10 → ROI ≈ −100%); bands 4–5 (the favorites) are strongly positive (+9.6 /
+14.0 pts raw). The DODGE is the **non-favorite residue** of the fleet's tennis
moneylines — longshot overpricing plus ROI amplification at low entries. It sharpens
rule 1 (bet consensus favorites only); it does NOT say "avoid tennis": favorite-tennis
is +12.7% ROI with LB −0.1% (misses the bar by a hair, see nominations). Both DODGE
cells are K2-stable and LODO-consistent in sign.

### The frequency × reliability frontier

Reliability (realizable-ROI LB) and volume overlap in exactly one place: **favorite's
favorite-band slices at 10–20 ev/day** (horizon<6h LB +6.8% at 20.5/d; opp≥1 LB +10.1%
at 13.5/d; σ≤0.04† LB +7.6% at 13.5/d). The highest-LB cells elsewhere are
low-frequency (elite_fresh_fav tennis LB +10.9% at 0.5/d). The reliably-negative mass
sits in strict's non-favorite residue at high frequency (moneyline-all-bands 37/d,
LB-UB fully negative). Crypto: consensus strategies still essentially never fire there
(the blind pool is crypto-rich; P1–P3 crypto cells are near-empty) — the standing
frequency observation, quantified, not a bug.

### The single change that most raises expected $/day without lowering the bar

**None of the pre-listed candidates clears the bar today; the honest answer is a
behavioral one plus two pre-registered forward reads.**
- Adding strict's 0.65–0.80 band to the alert set: NEUTRAL (ROI LB < 0). Not supported.
- A tennis-moneyline-only variant: favorite-tennis misses by a hair (LB −0.1%);
  nominated for a forward read (below), not for building.
- Excluding a DODGE cell from `favorite`: favorite HAS no reliably-negative slice —
  restriction can never raise its $/day. The DODGE cells live in the un-alerted strict
  residue; the actionable consequence is **never act on non-favorite tennis/moneyline
  signals from the fleet stream** — which is rule 1 made quantitative.
- What DID move $/day: nothing structural. The winners are already the right
  granularity at today's N (a K3-flavored conclusion for the volume question, even
  though 45 cells survive FDR for the reliability question).

## Nominations (pre-registered forward confirmation — NOT promotion, NOT new variants)

**Zero new StrategyDefs** — both nominations are re-reads of slices fully derivable from
stored at-fire data, so they cost no Bonferroni slot in the strategy family and leave
the alert path untouched (K4).

1. **favorite ∩ opposition≥1 ("contested favorite")** — claim to confirm: it carries
   ≈ all of favorite's $/day (+$228/d of ≈ +$242/d) at ~2× the per-bet ROI (capital
   efficiency, not new volume). Evaluation: first read at **30 NEW events** fired after
   2026-07-02 16:45 UTC, then every +15; confirm ⇔ matched surplus LB > 3% ∧
   selection-null p ≤ 0.01 ∧ ≥2 regimes > 0 (D7 equivalents on forward data) ∧ the
   opp≥1 − opp=0 difference > 0 at 95% (today the difference is NOT itself established;
   only the cell clears).
2. **favorite ∩ tennis** — today +12.7% ROI, LB −0.1% (misses by a hair), Wimbledon
   still accruing. Same evaluation points and bar. If it confirms, THEN a
   tennis-moneyline variant discussion is earned; not before.

Re-run trigger for the whole study: **+7 days or +300 new fleet events, whichever
first** (and once after the World Cup final for the soccer cells).

## What was deliberately NOT done

- No promotion, no alerting change, no env change, no real money (K4). No new silent
  variants (both nominations are derivable re-reads).
- No per-cell threshold tuning, no composite ranker over slice features, no
  dodge-by-small-N (all rejected-approaches honored).
- The horizon dimension is a proxy (resolved_at is processing time): "<6h" ≈ live/
  same-day matches. Acting on it at fire requires the market's scheduled end time,
  which IS knowable — but the slice as measured is not a StrategyDef param today.
