## Themes
- Honesty/measurement infra is the real near-term ROI, not new market signals. The five 'viable' verdicts are all accounting instruments (blind-band-benchmark, cluster-robust-dedup, correlation-effective-N, cross-venue-resolution-truth, depth-confirmed-dutch). Build the denominator + the promotion gate first; almost every 'edge' is only knowable through them.
- Favorite-longshot bias is the universal confound. Nearly every strategy that scores AVG(outcome_won - entry_price) can be gamed by loading favorites. Surplus-over-blind-by-band is the single control that recurs in 20+ entries; until it exists, no edge claim is interpretable.
- Multiple-comparisons / optional-stopping is the second systemic leak. Ten live variants + arbitrarily-many replayed param-sets + per-segment argmax all inflate family-wise false-promotion far above nominal. Every promotion-adjacent strategy needs FDR + always-valid confidence sequences + a distinct-EVENT (not raw-signal) N floor.
- Mid-price scoring is a phantom-edge generator across microstructure and cross-venue. Spreads, especially on thin Kalshi books, exceed almost any timing gain; the alert/paper-only constraint means anything whose payoff is sub-spread execution timing is structurally unharvestable. Score everything at executable bid/ask net of fees.
- Cross-venue's make-or-break is resolution concordance, not divergence magnitude. The widest, most persistent gaps are disproportionately non-fungible contracts (draw/ET/penalties/void rules). The infra that filters mismatch may REVEAL the divergence family is mostly dead rather than unlock it - so it must carry an explicit kill-criterion.
- Order-flow / news co-movement confound dominates every lead-lag and taker-flow idea (PM and Kalshi). Raw hit-rate >50% is expected with zero exploitable edge because news moves everything; only the news-stripped, executable-priced residual counts, and it is usually small and fast.
- Many surviving 'edges' are honestly INDETERMINATE-BY-POWER, mirroring the Foresight arc: real-but-tiny effects whose CIs straddle break-even on achievable N. The correct posture is pre-registered floors + silent forward-tracking, not promotion.
- Kalshi-native and scraped families are the weakest tier: no public positions means copy-consensus doesn't transfer, microstructure is unharvestable under paper-only, and scraping is paid/ToS-gray/identity-blind. Lean cross-venue (free, mirrored World Cup markets) instead of Kalshi-solo.
