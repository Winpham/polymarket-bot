# RESIDUAL SCAN — is per-sport conditioning progressive or regressive at realizable entry?

Compares the flat champion `favorite` book vs the arm's fired (non-tournament) subset vs the
discarded (soft-tournament) subset on belief-blind skill, within-subset selection-null, and
**realizable ROI (entry_ask, corrected fee)**. Reuses the committed instruments.

| population | nEv | raw skill | LB(pool) | null p | realizable ROI(ask) (cov) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| FULL favorite book (flat, sport-agnostic) | 153 | +4.5% | +0.5% | 0.002 | +1.2% (42%, n=185) |
| ARM non-tournament subset (mlb,nba/cbb,esports,…) | 31 | +10.4% | +1.7% | 0.058 | -9.1% (31%, n=12) |
| DISCARDED tournament subset (soccer,tennis) | 120 | +2.8% | -1.2% | 0.077 | +1.9% (43%, n=173) |

**Reading:** if the FULL flat book is belief-blind-significant and realizable-positive while
the non-tournament subset is not, per-sport conditioning SUBTRACTS realizable value — the
efficient-market skill is un-harvestable through thin-book spreads, and the realizable money
lives in the soft-but-liquid tournament cells the arm discards. See BYSPORT-VERDICT.md.
